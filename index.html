Why choose us

Four reasons Gauteng businesses choose Khanya

Load-shedding is South Africa's reality. We turn it into your competitive advantage.

Silent Backup

No diesel fumes, no 3 a.m. generator noise. Battery systems engage the instant grid power fails — zero interruption for your staff or customers.

Lower Monthly Bills

Generate your own electricity during the day, draw from batteries at night. Most clients cut their Eskom bill by 60–80% within the first year.

Fast Installation

Most commercial systems are fully installed and commissioned within 48 hours — minimal disruption to your operations.

Certified & Insured

All installations comply with SANS 10142 and are signed off by a registered electrician. Full workmanship warranty backed by public liability insurance.

What we offer

Tailored power solutions for your business

From a small retail shop to a multi-site operation — we size the right system for you.

Solar PV Systems

High-efficiency monocrystalline panels mounted on your roof or carport structure. Sized to offset your daytime load and charge your battery bank.

  • Commercial-grade tier-1 panels
  • Optimal tilt & shading analysis
  • 25-year performance warranty

Battery Storage

LiFePO₄ lithium battery banks store excess solar energy so you stay powered during outages, peak-tariff periods, and overnight.

  • 6 000+ cycle lifespan
  • Scalable modular design
  • Remote monitoring app

Hybrid Inverters

Intelligent hybrid inverters seamlessly switch between solar, battery, and grid — keeping critical loads online regardless of the source.

  • Sub-20ms switchover
  • Grid-feed & self-consumption modes
  • Load-shedding schedule sync

UPS & Backup Systems

Standalone UPS and critical-load backup panels for businesses that need select circuits protected without a full solar retrofit.

  • Protects fridges, POS, WiFi
  • Quiet & compact units
  • Easy future solar upgrade
